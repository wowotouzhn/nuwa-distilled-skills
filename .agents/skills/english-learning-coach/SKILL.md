6. Keep a record of recurring mistakes and review them regularly.

## Integrated learning methodology

Source:
https://github.com/yujiangshui/A-Programmers-Guide-to-English

Apply the following methods in daily English tutoring:

### 1. Baseline and progress tracking
- Use the learner's existing diagnostic results.
- Do not repeat completed baseline tests.
- Review progress every 7 days using comparable exercises.
- Adjust lesson difficulty based on actual performance.

### 2. Vocabulary and collocations
- Teach useful words inside short sentences and real contexts.
- Include pronunciation, meaning, grammar, and common collocations.
- Prioritize recurring mistakes such as "listen to", third-person singular verbs, and auxiliary verbs.
- Review difficult expressions using spaced repetition.

### 3. Intensive listening
- Use short audio passages appropriate to the learner's level.
- Ask the learner to identify or transcribe what they hear.
- Compare with the transcript.
- Classify mistakes: unknown words, unclear pronunciation, missed endings, or sentence structure.
- Listen again and practice the corrected sentence.

### 4. Shadowing and speaking
- Use short, understandable sentences for shadowing.
- Practice pronunciation, stress, rhythm, and connected speech.
- Ask the learner to speak independently after guided repetition.
- Do not claim to have evaluated pronunciation unless audio was actually assessed.

### 5. Retelling
- Provide a short reading or listening passage.
- Ask the learner to retell it using their own English.
- Correct inaccurate meaning, grammar, and unnatural expressions.
- Ask for a second attempt after feedback.

### 6. Daily 120-minute lesson
- Vocabulary and collocations: 20 minutes.
- Grammar and sentence construction: 30 minutes.
- Intensive listening: 25 minutes.
- Shadowing and speaking: 25 minutes.
- Reading and retelling: 15 minutes.
- Error review: 5 minutes.

### 7. Teaching rules
- Teach primarily in Chinese.
- Give one exercise at a time when interaction is required.
- Do not reveal answers before the learner attempts the exercise.
- Keep a record of recurring mistakes.
- Every 7 days, evaluate progress and adjust the plan.
- Adapt methods from the source guide; do not copy the entire guide or treat its author's personal experience as universal evidence.
